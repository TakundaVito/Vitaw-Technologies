Vitaw Technologies is a global technology and engineering company headquartered in Zimbabwe. We bring software, AI, automation and physical engineering together for clients around the world. We believe these disciplines should operate as connected parts of a complete system.

One connected approach

Our work spans software development, artificial intelligence, electronics, industrial automation, IoT and engineering design. We start by understanding the problem, then connect the right disciplines to design, build and deploy a complete solution.

Our blue loop identity represents continuity, motion, engineering, transformation and interconnected systems.
