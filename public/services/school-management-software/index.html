School management software.
Built around your school.

Vitaw Technologies develops mobile-first school management systems, student information systems and school ERP software for education organisations across Africa and worldwide.

Connect school administration with useful software

School teams need information they can find, understand and act on. We work with administrators and staff to map everyday tasks, identify disconnected records and define the workflows a school management platform should support.

Choose the modules your institution needs

We can scope online admissions, student records, attendance management, fee administration, examinations and results, timetables, staff access, management reporting, parent portals and school communication. Modules and integrations are agreed before implementation.

Mobile-first access and practical deployment

We identify which tasks staff, parents and learners need to perform on phones and which require a desktop workspace. The solution can include responsive web access, Android or iOS apps, role-based permissions, secure data handling and connectivity-aware workflows.

Implementation, migration and training

A successful student management system depends on clean data and confident users. We plan migration, testing, access levels, backups, onboarding and support with the institution. Public forms should never be used to send real student records or sensitive personal information.

For schools in Africa and beyond

Vitaw is headquartered in Zimbabwe and operates globally. We design education management software around each institution’s processes rather than forcing every school into one rigid template.
