School management software.
Built around your school.

Vitaw Technologies develops school management software for education organisations across Africa and worldwide.

Connect school administration with useful software

School teams need information they can find, understand and act on. We work with your administrators and staff to map everyday tasks, identify disconnected records and define the workflows a school management system should support.

Define the modules your school needs

During scoping, we can discuss requirements such as admissions and student records, attendance, fees administration, academic reporting, staff access and communication with parents. We agree the modules and integrations with your team before implementation.

Mobile-first access and practical deployment

Tell us which tasks staff, parents or learners need to perform on phones and which need a desktop workspace. We consider responsive interfaces, access permissions, data handling, existing records and connectivity needs as part of the project design.

For schools in Africa and beyond

Vitaw is headquartered in Zimbabwe and operates globally. We scope the software around your institution’s processes and delivery requirements, including data migration, staff onboarding and support where agreed.

Request a school software consultation

Share your school type, approximate user numbers, current systems, required workflows and preferred timeline. Please do not send student records or sensitive personal information through the public enquiry form. Pricing, delivery and support are confirmed after reviewing the scope.
