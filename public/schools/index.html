QUESTIONS FROM SCHOOLS

Before you get started.

Can the system work without internet?

The platform is web-based and requires internet for normal use. The deployed application preserves some mark drafts in the current browser during a temporary connection loss, but it should not be treated as a fully offline system.

Can existing records be imported from Excel?

Record migration is assessed during onboarding. Send a sample spreadsheet without sensitive live data so we can confirm the format, cleanup and import work required.

How is school information protected?

The platform uses authenticated, role-aware workspaces. Detailed security, retention and backup arrangements are confirmed during onboarding; access should always be limited to authorised school users.

Can parents and learners access the system?

The deployed application includes separate parent and learner areas. Access and available information depend on the school configuration and assigned account.

What training is provided?

The US$250 onboarding fee covers setup and configuration. Optional staff training is scoped and quoted separately according to team size, location and delivery format.

Are SMS or WhatsApp credits included?

No messaging-credit allowance is included in the published subscription price. Any third-party messaging service and usage charges are confirmed separately.

Can it integrate with payment services?

Payment integrations depend on the provider, school requirements and technical review. No specific payment method is promised until it has been confirmed in the agreed scope.
