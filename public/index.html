PROJECT QUESTIONS

START WITH
THE RIGHT QUESTIONS.

What does Vitaw Technologies do?

Vitaw Technologies is a global technology and engineering company headquartered in Zimbabwe, serving clients worldwide and working across software development, websites, mobile applications, AI assistants, industrial automation, IoT, electronics and engineering design.

Can you build an AI chatbot for my business?

Yes. We scope website assistants and business automation around your information and workflows. Tell us what the assistant should answer, which systems it must connect to and when it should hand over to a person.

Where can I see examples of your website work?

Our website portfolio includes Quadratic Hygienics, Waima Autocare, Boreline Solutions, Virtuous Trends, Nheyotech, Carstie, Chase The Transporter, Eastgate Furnitures, Engirvine and Morden Masonry.

Do you work with clients outside Zimbabwe?

Yes. Vitaw Technologies operates globally. Our headquarters are in Zimbabwe, and we work with businesses and organisations around the world. Project scope, collaboration and delivery arrangements are agreed with each client.

Does Vitaw build school management software?

Yes. Vitaw Technologies develops school management software. We review your school’s administration, reporting and communication requirements, then agree the modules, integrations and implementation scope with your team.

How do I request a quote?

Email info@vitaw.co.zw, call +263 712 251 740 or use the project inquiry form. Share your goal, required features, preferred timeline and any existing systems. Scope, pricing and delivery dates are agreed after reviewing the brief.
