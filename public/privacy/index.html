Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Vitaw Technologies operates this website and provides technology and engineering services worldwide from its headquarters in Zimbabwe. This policy describes information handled through this website.

Information you provide

When you send a project enquiry, we collect the details you submit: your name, company, email address, phone number, project type, budget range, project description and any supporting document you choose to attach. Please do not submit passwords, identity documents or confidential third-party information.

How information is used

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, discuss requirements, prepare proposals and manage related project communication. Technical request information, such as an IP address, may be used for security and abuse prevention. Enquiries and attachments are stored through the website’s hosting infrastructure.

Hosting and external services

The website is hosted with Hostinger. Hosting infrastructure may process technical request data to deliver and protect the site. Links to project websites, email and WhatsApp take you to external services whose own privacy practices apply. Information may be processed outside your country through these providers.

Cookies and browser storage

Administrative sign-in uses a session cookie. Hosting infrastructure may use security cookies or browser checks. This policy does not give consent for advertising tracking; any future tracking that requires consent should be accompanied by an appropriate choice.

Retention and your choices

We keep enquiry information for responding to and managing the request and associated business records. You can contact us to ask what information we hold about your enquiry, request a correction or ask for deletion. We will review requests, verify identity where necessary and explain any information that must be retained.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@vitaw.co.zw. We may update this policy as the website and services change.
