Seven business processes worth automating first

Good first automations are frequent, rule-guided and easy to measure. They create visible relief without putting the business at unnecessary risk.

1. Website enquiries

Capture every enquiry in one place, acknowledge it immediately, classify the request and route it to the right person. An AI assistant can answer approved common questions while handing complex matters to staff.

2. Sales follow-up

Create reminders, prepare personalised follow-up drafts and keep opportunity stages current. Automation prevents promising conversations from disappearing inside inboxes.

3. Customer support triage

Classify requests by topic and urgency, suggest knowledge-base answers and alert a person when sentiment, value or risk requires attention.

4. Invoice and payment administration

Generate invoices from approved records, send reminders and reconcile status. Keep human approval for refunds, payment changes and unusual transactions.

5. Recurring reports

Bring data together on a schedule, validate it and deliver a concise operational summary. AI can explain changes, while the underlying figures remain traceable.

6. Internal approvals

Route leave, purchasing, content and operational requests through clear approval steps with notifications and an auditable record.

7. Document intake

Extract relevant fields from standard forms or documents, validate required information and create a review task when confidence is low.

Choose one workflow with a clear owner and baseline. Design the exception path before automating the normal path.
